Why we exist

Small and medium businesses carry the Indian economy. Their owners spend much of the day on work that takes hours and demands precision: marketing, follow-ups, coordination, paperwork.

That work is too important to skip and too costly to staff well. So it gets done late, done badly, or not at all.

We build AI agents that take it on, each one focused on a single job and built to do it reliably, so owners can spend their time growing the business.

Why I started Zuman

For nine years I ran a contracting business. Digital marketing was one of our best sources of leads, and one of the hardest things to keep going. I had to coordinate camera crews' schedules, chase editors for proofs, and depend on marketers who knew social media but not our trade.

So I started solving it myself. Using AI tools, I built a working marketing system on my phone that did the job better than the process it replaced.

Then a bigger question took hold: what if every small business in India had the same thing? If the hard, time-consuming work could be handled with accuracy, owners could spend their time growing.

That question became Zuman Intelligence, and Vyabix is our first answer.
