Questions

Good to know

Do I need to install an app?

Yes, once. You install the Vyabix app to set up your business and connect your Google, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and X pages. After that, day-to-day work runs in WhatsApp: ideas, approvals, enquiries and reports. The app also has a full dashboard for advanced users who want detailed analytics, a content calendar and bulk scheduling.

Will anything be posted without my permission?

Never. Every post is sent to you as a preview first. It goes live only after you tap Approve. You can also edit the caption or skip the idea.

Which platforms do you post to?

Google My Business, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and X. You can switch any of them off by sending a message.

How do the daily ideas work?

Each morning we send one simple task based on your industry, the season, upcoming festivals and what got the most enquiries for you recently. It takes 1 to 2 minutes to do.

How soon will I see results?

Most businesses see more profile visits in the first 2 weeks and a steady rise in enquiries within 30 to 60 days of posting regularly.

I don't have a website. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Every plan includes a free one-page website with your photos, timings, map and a WhatsApp order button.

What are "wins"?

When a post brings you an order, booking or call, just tell us in the chat. We record it, show it in your monthly report and suggest more ideas like the ones that worked.
