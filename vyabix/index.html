Questions

Good to know

Do I need to install an app?

Yes, once. You install the Vyabix app to set up your business and connect your Google, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and X pages. After that, daily work runs in WhatsApp. Advanced users also get a full dashboard.

Will anything be posted without my permission?

Never. Every post is sent to you as a preview first. It goes live only after you tap Approve. You can also edit the caption or skip the idea.

How do I know Vyabix is working?

At sign-up we study your existing profiles and record your starting numbers: profile visits, reviews and rating, posting frequency, followers and enquiries. Every monthly report shows your growth against that baseline.

How soon will I see results?

Most businesses see more profile visits in the first 2 weeks and a steady rise in enquiries within 30 to 60 days of posting regularly.
