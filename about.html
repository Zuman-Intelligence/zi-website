Built by a business owner, for business owners.

Zuman Intelligence was founded in Tirupur, Tamil Nadu, to give small businesses the kind of help that large companies get from whole teams.

Our story

Zuman Intelligence was founded by Sivaprakasam Nagarajan, who also runs Universal Associates, a turnkey construction and fit-out company.

Running a business on the ground showed him the gap: small business owners are experts at their trade, but marketing, follow-ups and admin eat up their day. Large companies solve this with teams. Small ones go without.

Zuman exists to close that gap with AI agents that do the work, not just advise on it. Read Shiva's note

The name

Zuman (said ZYOO-man) joins Z, for the future, with human.

Our tagline is One intelligence. Infinite possibilities. It describes how we build: one intelligence powering many products, starting with Kural.

Our agents work alongside people, taking on complex, time-consuming work and doing it accurately, so owners can focus on what only they can do. The friendly agent face in our logo says the same thing.

Our principles

  • Real results over features

    An agent is only useful if it moves the business forward: more visibility, more customers, more time.

  • Affordable for the smallest shop

    If a neighbourhood salon can't afford it, we haven't built it right.

  • Local first

    Indian languages, Indian trades and Indian ways of doing business are the starting point, not an afterthought.
