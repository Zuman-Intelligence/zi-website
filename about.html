About us

Built by a business owner,

Our story

Zuman Intelligence was founded in Tirupur, Tamil Nadu, by Sivaprakasam Nagarajan, who also runs Universal Associates, a turnkey construction and fit-out company.

Running a business on the ground showed him the gap: small business owners are experts at their trade, but marketing, follow-ups and admin eat up their day. Large companies solve this with teams. Small ones go without.

Zuman exists to close that gap with AI agents that do the work, not just advise on it.

The name

Zuman (said ZYOO-man) joins Z, for the future, with human.

It stands for AI with a human touch: agents that work alongside people, taking on repetitive work so owners can do what only they can. The friendly agent face in our logo says the same thing.
